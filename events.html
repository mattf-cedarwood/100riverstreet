Rent the Great Hall

Springfield's gathering place. 6,592 square feet on the Black River — for weddings, markets, fundraisers, meetings, and everything in between.

The Space

One Room, Many Uses

The Great Hall is a wide-open 6,592 SF space in the heart of the historic mill campus — high ceilings, natural light, and direct load-in access. It seats a crowd for a wedding or a town meeting, and it opens up for a craft market, an art show, or a fundraiser.

Rentals start at a three-hour block. Tables, chairs, and a full AV setup are available as add-ons. Non-profit, municipal, school, and church organizations rent at half the commercial rate.

The Details

Booking Terms

  • The cleaning charge is a deposit, not a fee. It's collected with your final balance and returned in full within 10 business days if the Hall is left broom-clean, trash removed, and undamaged.
  • Non-profit rate requires proof of 501(c)(3) status, or municipal, school, or church affiliation.
  • Payment. 50% of the space rental holds your date. The balance and the cleaning deposit are due 7 days before the event.
  • Cancellation. More than 30 days out: full refund less a $100 administrative fee. 15–30 days: 50% of space rental retained. 14 days or less: space rental non-refundable. The cleaning deposit is always returned.
  • Insurance. A certificate of insurance ($1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate) naming 100 River Street Venture, LLC as additional insured is due 7 days before the event.
  • Alcohol requires a Vermont liquor permit and a licensed, insured bartender. No self-service.
  • House rules. No smoking, open flame, confetti, or glitter. Nothing attached to walls, floors, or historic fabric. Amplified sound ends at 10:00 p.m.
  • Every rental is confirmed with a signed Great Hall Event Rental Agreement.
