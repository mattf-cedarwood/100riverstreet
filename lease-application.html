Lease Application

For tenants ready to move forward on a space at 100 River Street. This gives us what we need to prepare a lease. Most applicants finish it in 10–15 minutes.

Before you start. Have handy: your legal entity name and EIN, the space and term you want, two trade references, and your insurance agent's contact. Anything you don't know yet, leave blank — we'll fill the gaps together.

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or card details on this form. If a personal credit check or personal financial statement is needed, we'll arrange that separately through a secure channel.

1. Business Information

The entity that will sign the lease.

2. Primary Contact

Who we work with day to day.

3. Space & Term Requested

What you want to lease and for how long.

4. Intended Use & Operations

How the space will actually be used. This drives permitting, insurance and the use clause.

5. Build-Out & Utilities

What the space needs before you can open.

6. References

Names and contacts only — please don't enter account numbers.

7. Insurance

9. Certification

Submitted directly to the ownership and leasing team. Not shared outside it.
